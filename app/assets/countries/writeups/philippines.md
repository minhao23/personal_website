## Cities Visited

- Cebu

I visited Cebu in May 2025.

The highlight of the trip was the diving. Gliding underwater next to sea turtles was already special, but seeing whale sharks up close was something else entirely. Being in the water beside an animal that size is humbling, and it's an experience I won't forget anytime soon.

All in all, it was one of my more adrenaline-packed trips, with plenty of time spent in the water and very little sitting still.

The heat caught me off guard. I thought nothing could be hotter than Singapore, but Cebu has far fewer trees, which means much less shade, and the sun felt almost unbearable at times. If you go, sunblock is a must, and so is a hat and plenty of water.
