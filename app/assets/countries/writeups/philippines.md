## Cities Visited

- Cebu

I visited Cebu in May 2025.

The highlights of the trip were the diving. It was especially incredible to see sea turtles and even whale sharks.

All in all, it was one of the more adrenaline-packed trips.

Surprisingly hot. Despite thinking nothing could be hotter than Singapore, the fact that Cebu has far fewer trees made it especially unbearable. Sunblock is a necessity.
