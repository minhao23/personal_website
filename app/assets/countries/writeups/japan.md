## Cities Visited

- Kyoto
- Osaka
- Nara
- Sapporo
- Otaru
- Kobe

Japan was one of those trips where every stop felt like its own world. From the calmer rhythm of Kyoto and Nara to the busier energy of Osaka, and then all the way up to Sapporo and Otaru, it never really felt repetitive.

What I liked most was how easy it was to move between very different moods in a single trip. One moment it felt traditional and quiet in the streets of Kyoto, and the next minute you're staring at the bright lights of Dontonburi in Osaka. 

Of course the highlights of the Kansai was Nara's deer park. Roaming freely with deers was a pretty unique experience, and its something I highly recommend doing a day trip for.

Otaru and Sapporo were pretty unique places in Hokkaido to visit. In all honestly, Otaru had nothing much to do, it was a quiet little town, but for those who need a break from the bustling energy of Sapporo, it's a good place to visit.
