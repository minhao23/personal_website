## Cities Visited

- Kyoto
- Osaka
- Nara
- Sapporo
- Otaru
- Kobe

Japan was one of those trips where every stop felt like its own world. From the calmer rhythm of Kyoto and Nara to the busier energy of Osaka, and then all the way up to Sapporo and Otaru, it never really felt repetitive.

What I liked most was how easy it was to move between very different moods in a single trip. One moment it felt traditional and quiet, and the next it felt bright, dense, and full of motion in the way only Japan can.
