## Cities Visited

- Chiang Mai
- Phuket

Thailand is the land of smiles, and for most of the time I was there, I was definitely smiling. From the pristine beaches of Phuket to the soul-awakening views of Chiang Mai, there was always something to see and something to do.

I went to Chiang Mai in late 2019 as part of a service learning trip with my junior college. Chiang Mai was a rather quiet and religious area, and a good place to go to learn about the culture of Buddhism. The kids we helped were really well behaved, and honestly it really put things into perspective about how fortunate we were to be born in Singapore, where all public schools are well build structures.

Phuket was more of a vacation with friends, and there's something to be done there for everyone. The beach was probably one of the best ones I have seen, and there were many activies to be done. Of course, the highlights were the nightlife. Although it was a bit noisy, it was still a good experience to have, and it really was something that was unique to Thailand.
