## Cities Visited

- Chiang Mai
- Phuket

Thailand is the land of smiles, and for most of the time I was there, I was definitely smiling. From the pristine beaches of Phuket to the soul-awakening views of Chiang Mai, there was always something to see and something to do.

I went to Chiang Mai in late 2019 as part of a service learning trip with my junior college.

Phuket was more of a vacation with friends.
