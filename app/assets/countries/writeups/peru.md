## Cities Visited

- Lima
- Cusco

18,000 kilometers away from home, Peru was, and likely will be, the furthest I will ever be from Singapore.

I still remember the day I sat with the people I eventually went to Peru with in early 2025. My god, the amount of effort I put into convincing them. You'd think I could have had a career in sales.

But the place was truly something special. The minute you go down that one road in Lima, you truly realise you're in another world. The sea, the mountains, the steep cliffs that trace the coast, the combination of everything made for special viewing. 

Cusco was a whole different beast for me. Being 3.5km up in the air was not something I was ready for, and it was slightly challenging. But wow, the views were just amazing. 

And finally, unfortunately I did not get to see Machu Picchu, so I hope that changes in the future.
