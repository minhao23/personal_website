## Cities Visited

- Seoul

I visited Seoul in Decemeber 2025, on the way back from my exchange. The first thing you need to know about Seoul is that unfortunately, I did not enjoy it that much. For the most part, it was much too cold at the tail end of December. At double digit negatives, I was very much underdressed for the occasion. Aside from that, there were definitely quite a few unsavoury experiences with the locals, from the Uber driver who was probably scolding me for carrying two luggages, to probably the most infuriating airport experience, I can't say I left the country with the best impression.

With that out of the way. Seoul was what you'd expect from a modern city where people are extremely resourceful and clever. Daiso's $1 heatpack was something that was incredibly useful, and having that on me in that weather was probably one of life's great experiences. 

The food was pretty awesome, and honestly Korean fried chicken is probably one of the best renditions of fried chicken out there.

In all honestly, don't let my negative opinions affect your opinion of Korea, if you love beauty products or you are extremely interested in the culture, 100% do go and see it for yourself, BUT if you have no interest in either of those, then open the world map, look at Japan, look at China, look at Taiwan, and probably go there instead.
