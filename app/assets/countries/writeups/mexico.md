## Cities Visited

- Mexico City

Mexico, much like Peru is one of those places that if you told a Singaporean guy you've visted, they would probably think you are crazy.

The best part about Mexico City is the food. Like WOW. Genuinely had some of the best budget sushi and Mala Hotpot while I was there, and it was pretty funny busting out Chinese in the middle of CDMX. And as for the Mexican food, theres is probably nothing that you can get in Singapore that can come close to the quality. There are some cuisines where dishes are more about presenting food in a simple and minimalistic way and allowing the ingredients to shine, and Mexican food was one of those. 

A simple bowl of guac or salsa, or even to a grilled cactus (??), you can really experience the difference in the quality of the ingredients.

The people were really nice and friendly, it was a fantastic experience talking to them, and you could see them really putting in the effort to help us, even with the language barrier.
