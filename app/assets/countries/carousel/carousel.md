### when to use
- when you are building the travel modal.
### style guide
when looking through the photos, they will come either landscape or portrait.
- if the photo is landscape, then the carousel will be landscape, and we will scale the photo such that it extends the entire width.
- if the photo is portrait, then the carousel will be portrait, and we will scale the photo such that 2 photos fit in the the width. scale the image to fit best.

- if possible, make sure the first image is always landscape, and space the image our over text.
- Get the write up from each country from the <country.md>. There should be paragraph spacing, and make sure the images are spaced out amongst the text, to make it look like a nice travel page.
